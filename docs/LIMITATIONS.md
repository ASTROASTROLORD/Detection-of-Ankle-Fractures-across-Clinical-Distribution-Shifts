# Limitations

Performance depends on image quality, projection, acquisition domain,
annotation quality, and the declared IoU and score thresholds. Boxes from
different images do not share a coordinate system; patient-level metrics are
therefore fracture-presence metrics rather than patient-level box mAP.

The public package contains no patient data and no claim of clinical
effectiveness. Users must establish data governance, external validity, and
appropriate regulatory controls for their intended context.
